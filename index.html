Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between psychiatry and counseling?

Psychiatry is medical care that can include diagnosis and medication management, while counseling focuses on talk therapy and skill building. Many people benefit from both.


What is EMDR therapy?

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is a structured therapy used for trauma and PTSD. It helps the brain process distressing memories so they feel less overwhelming.


Do you treat ADHD?

We offer behavioral treatment for ADHD and medication management for adults. During your evaluation, we will discuss what support is most suitable for you.


What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards. Please call to ask about fees and scheduling.
